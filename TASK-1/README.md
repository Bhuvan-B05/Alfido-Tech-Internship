# 🍴 Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis

## Alfido Tech Internship – Task 1

### 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Zomato restaurant dataset to uncover insights about restaurant ratings, cuisine preferences, pricing patterns, customer engagement, and location-based trends.

The objective is to perform data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization to understand factors influencing restaurant performance and customer satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
* Analyze restaurant ratings and customer votes
* Identify popular cuisines and restaurant hotspots
* Study the relationship between pricing and ratings
* Create meaningful visualizations
* Generate business recommendations for food-tech platforms

---

## 📊 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Zomato Restaurant Dataset

The dataset contains information such as:

* Restaurant Name
* Location
* Restaurant Type
* Cuisines
* Ratings
* Customer Votes
* Cost for Two People
* Online Ordering Availability
* Table Booking Availability

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. Most restaurants have ratings between 3.5 and 4.2.
2. Restaurant concentration is highest in major food hubs.
3. North Indian and Chinese cuisines are the most popular.
4. Higher pricing does not necessarily lead to higher ratings.
5. Restaurants with greater customer engagement tend to receive more votes and better visibility.
